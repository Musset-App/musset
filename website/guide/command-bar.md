## One key for everything

Musset keeps its interface out of the page. There is no toolbar full of buttons, because there is one place that holds them all: press [[⌘K]] and type what you want.

The field says **Type a command or search…**. Move with [[↑]] and [[↓]], press [[↩]] to open, [[Esc]] to close. Typing matches any part of a name, ignores accents and capitals, and takes several words in any order: *ferry morn* finds the scene *Morning crossing* in the chapter *The ferry*.

![The command bar](command-bar)

## What you can reach

**Go to.** Every chapter of the book, with its status; every scene that has a title; and every chapter of your *other* books, so you can jump to the novel you set aside without closing this one.

**Bible.** Every sheet: a character, a place, a document. Type a name and press Return.

**Commands.** Everything the menus hold, with its shortcut beside it, which is also how you learn the shortcuts. And a few things that live only here:

| Type | To |
|---|---|
| *milestone* | Set a milestone |
| *track* | Start a parallel draft of the book |
| *history* | Open the book's history |
| *version* | Start a new version of the chapter |
| *share* or *invite* | Share the book |
| *minimap*, *line numbers* | Show them |
| *light*, *dark* | Change the appearance |
| *overview*, *synopsis*, *sources* | Open those pages |
| *layout* | Save or recall an arrangement of panes |

**Critique.** If you use [the editor inside](/guide/ai-editor/), its readings of the open chapter are here: style and problems, tics, continuity, what to cut, the opening and the ending, facts to check.

**Text.** Type two letters or more and the last row offers to search the whole book for them.

## Open something beside what you are writing

[[⇧⌘O]] (**Open Beside…**) is the same bar with one difference: what you choose opens in the next pane, beside your chapter. It is the quick way to put a character's sheet or the previous chapter next to the page. See [panes, tabs and windows](/guide/panes-and-windows/).

## From vi and Emacs

In [vi mode](/guide/vi-keys/), `:` opens the command bar. In [Emacs mode](/guide/emacs-keys/), [[⌥X]] does.

## A habit worth taking

When you wonder whether Musset can do something, press [[⌘K]] and type the word. If it exists, it is there, and its shortcut is shown for next time.

The bar belongs to the open book: on the All Books screen, open a book first.
