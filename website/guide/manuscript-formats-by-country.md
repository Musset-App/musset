## Why a manuscript does not look like a book

A submission manuscript is a working document. It is set in a plain face with wide spacing so that an editor can read it quickly, estimate its length at a glance and write between the lines. Each publishing culture settled on its own page, and on its own way of saying how long a text is.

Musset makes the manuscript of your book's language. Press [[⌘E]], choose **PDF**, and the first layout in the list is the one for your country.

## The formats at a glance

| Country | Layout in Musset | Page | Type and spacing | Length is given in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| United States | Standard manuscript (US Letter) | 8.5 × 11 in, 1 in margins | Times New Roman 12, double spaced | Words, rounded to the hundred |
| United Kingdom | Standard manuscript (A4) | A4, 1 in margins | Times New Roman 12, double spaced | Words, rounded to the hundred |
| France | Manuscript for French publishers | A4, 2.5 cm margins | 12 point, double spaced | Feuillets and signes |
| Germany, Switzerland | Manuscript in Normseiten (30 × 60) | A4 | Courier 12, 30 lines of 60 characters | Normseiten and Zeichen |
| Italy | Manuscript in cartelle (30 × 60) | A4 | Courier 12, 30 lines of 60 characters | Cartelle and battute |
| Spain, Latin America | Manuscript for Spanish publishers | A4 | Times New Roman 12, spacing 1.5 | Characters with spaces, and words |
| Portugal, Brazil | Manuscript for Portuguese or Brazilian publishers | A4 | Times New Roman 12, spacing 1.5 | Characters with spaces, and words |

Each has its own page in this guide: [standard manuscript format](/guide/standard-manuscript-format/) for English, [the Normseite](/guide/normseite/), [feuillets and signes](/guide/feuillet-and-signes/), and [the cartella](/guide/cartella-editoriale/).

## What every manuscript has

Whatever the country, Musset's manuscripts share the conventions an editor relies on:

- a **title page** with the title, your name, your contact details and the length of the text;
- a **header on every page** with your surname, the title and the page number, so loose pages can be put back in order;
- text **aligned on the left**, not justified, and **not hyphenated**;
- every paragraph **indented**, and no blank line between paragraphs;
- each chapter on **a new page**, its number above its title;
- single-sided pages.

Enter your contact details once, in the book's settings under **Contact (manuscript)**.

## How publishers count

Asked how long your book is, do not answer in pages: a page means nothing until the layout is fixed. Each country has its unit, and Musset prints the right figure on the title page.

| Country | The figure on the title page | How it is worked out |
|---|---|---|
| US, UK | *about 82,400 words* | Words, rounded to the nearest hundred |
| France | *329 feuillets (493,000 signes)* | Characters with spaces ÷ 1,500 |
| Germany | *ca. 274 Normseiten (493.000 Zeichen)* | Characters with spaces ÷ 1,800 |
| Italy | *circa 274 cartelle (493.000 battute)* | Characters with spaces ÷ 1,800 |
| Spain | *493.000 caracteres con espacios · 82.400 palabras* | Both |
| Portugal, Brazil | *493.000 caracteres com espaços · 82.400 palavras* | Both |

While you write, click the count at the bottom of the page to switch between words, characters, and characters with spaces. See [word count and goals](/guide/word-count-and-goals/).

## Submitting to another country

The manuscript follows the book's language. An American writing in English for a London agent sets the book's language to **English (UK)** in the book's settings: the manuscript becomes A4, and the typography follows British usage too.

To submit a translation, make it a book of its own, in its own language.

## Competitions and anonymous submissions

Many prizes require a manuscript without the author's name. In the export sheet, with a manuscript layout chosen, fill in **Pseudonym (prizes)**. The title page and the header then carry the pseudonym, and your contact details are left out.

> The exported file's **name** still contains the author's name from the book's settings. Rename the file before you send it to a competition that judges blind.

## The guidelines win

These are the conventions of each country, and they are what to send when nobody tells you otherwise. When an agent, a publisher or a prize publishes its own requirements, a typeface, a file type, a maximum length, follow those. If they ask for a Word file, export [Word](/guide/export-pdf-word-epub/#word) instead.
