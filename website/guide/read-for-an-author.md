## You received an invitation

An author invited you to read their book. You do not need to buy anything: a reader's account is free, and stays free.

You can read:

- on a **Mac**, in Musset;
- on an **iPhone or iPad**, in Musset Reader.

Open the link in the e-mail on the device you want to read on. It creates your account, or signs you in, and takes you to the book.

## What you are reading

You read a **milestone**: the book as the author chose to show it to you. They may go on writing; you will not see half-finished pages. When they share a newer draft, you are told, and your notes follow you to it.

The author may have shared only a part of the book. In that case your list of chapters shows that part.

## On an iPhone or iPad

Musset Reader shows the book set in pages, like a printed book. The menu at the top right holds the chapters, the text size and a night mode.

Select some text to mark it up:

| You choose | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Highlight** | Marks the passage in your colour |
| **Underline** | Underlines the passage |
| **Note** | Leaves a note for the author on the passage |
| **Suggest** | Proposes what the passage should say |

Every mark opens a conversation with the author. Tap a mark to see the thread, reply, or resolve it. The **Activity** tab shows the author's answers.

Musset Reader needs a connection: it reads the book from the author's account.

## On a Mac

1. Open Musset and sign in. The book is under **All Books** ([[⇧⌘H]]), **Shared with me**.
2. Click **Download**, then **Open**.

The book opens in Revise mode. That means you can read with a pen in hand:

- change the text directly, and your changes are kept as [corrections](/guide/track-changes/) the author will accept or reject;
- select a passage and leave a note for the author from the Notes panel ([[⌘2]]);
- highlight and comment with the toolbar above the text.

You cannot damage anything. Your work is kept apart from the author's text until they bring it in.

## Send your review

Notes and replies reach the author as you go. Changes to the text are sent when you decide:

1. Click **Share** in the title bar to open the book's page.
2. Add a word for the author if you like.
3. Click **Send my review**.

Send your review before you take a newer draft. When the author shares a new milestone, Musset offers **Read it**; what you had not sent stays behind.

## Good notes

The author asked for a reader, so a few habits make your reading more useful:

- Say where you stopped believing, or where you skimmed. That is worth more than a fix.
- Quote little and point precisely. A note on one sentence is easier to act on than a note on a page.
- Keep questions as questions. *Why does she stay?* tells the author what the page did not answer.

## Leaving

To stop reading a book, ask the author to stop sharing it. You can delete your account at any time from Musset Reader, under **Circle**; your notes go with it, and the books stay with their authors.
