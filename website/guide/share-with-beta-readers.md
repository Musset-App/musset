## How sharing works in Musset

Sending a manuscript usually means exporting a file, e-mailing it, and collecting comments from five people in five formats. Then you keep writing, and nobody's page numbers match any more.

In Musset, a reader opens your book in their own Musset, or in Musset Reader on an iPhone or iPad. Two rules keep it calm:

- **Readers read a milestone, never your desk.** You choose the state of the book each person reads. What you write afterwards stays yours until you share a newer [milestone](/guide/milestones/).
- **Nobody writes in your text.** Their changes arrive as corrections for you to accept or reject, and their remarks as conversations on the passage.

Readers do not pay: their account is free. Sharing needs [Musset Sync](/guide/musset-sync/) on your side, because the book has to be online to be read elsewhere.

## Invite someone

1. Open the book and click **Share** in the title bar, or choose **Invite Someone to This Book…** from the command bar ([[⌘K]]).
2. Click **Invite someone…** and enter their e-mail. They get a link; they do not need Musset yet.
3. Choose what they can do (see the roles below).
4. Choose what they read: a milestone, and the whole book or a part of it.
5. Say who they are to you: editor, beta reader, publisher, agent, translator.
6. Add a word for them if you like, and click **Send the invitation**.

If you have no milestone yet, Musset sets one named *First reading* when you send.

## Reader, editor or co-writer

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Reader** | Marks up the text and leaves notes |
| **Editor** | Proposes corrections and notes; you accept or reject each one |
| **Co-writer** | Writes in the text with you, and sees the book's documents |

Readers and editors read a milestone. A co-writer is different: they work on the manuscript itself, as it is. See [writing a book together](/guide/co-writing/).

You can change someone's role at any time in the list of people, and stop sharing with one click. A person you share one book with never sees your other books.

## Share only a part of the book

Chapters one to twelve are ready and the ending is not. Under **What they read**, choose:

- **The whole book**;
- **Chapters by status**, for example everything marked *Edited* or *Final*;
- **Chosen chapters**, from a checklist.

In Musset and in Musset Reader, that person's list of chapters shows only the part you chose. With the status option, a newer milestone brings them whatever has reached those statuses by then.

To change it later, open the flag menu beside their name and choose **Which Part of the Book…**.

## Give readers a newer draft

Your sharing page always tells you where things stand: which milestone each person reads, and how many words you have written since that nobody has seen.

When a new draft is ready:

1. Choose **Set a Milestone…** from the command bar.
2. Tick the people who should read it. Nobody is ticked unless you tick them.
3. Confirm. They are told by e-mail and in their Musset, and their notes follow them to the new text.

Everyone you did not tick keeps reading what they had. This is how an editor can be on the third draft while a late beta reader finishes the second.

## Read what they sent

**Conversations.** A reader's note sits on the passage it is about and opens a thread: you reply, they reply, one of you resolves it. Find them at the top of the Notes panel ([[⌘2]]), with a filter for the ones waiting for you. Conversations never change the text.

**Reviews.** When someone has changed the text itself, they send a review. It appears under **Reviews to read** on the sharing page. Click **Bring into my text** and their changes become [corrections](/guide/track-changes/) placed on your text as it is today. Passages you have rewritten since arrive as conversation entries instead, so nothing is forced onto a sentence that no longer exists.

**Activity.** In **All Books** ([[⇧⌘H]]), **Activity** lists what happened across your shared books. Under **Notifications**, choose whether e-mails come at once, grouped, or never.

## Your circle

Everyone you share with joins your **circle**, grouped by who they are to you. Next time, sharing another book with your editor is one click from the sharing page, with the role that fits them.
