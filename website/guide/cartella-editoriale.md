## Battute and cartelle

In Italian publishing the unit of length is the **battuta**: one keystroke, so one character, with spaces counted. A text is so many *battute spazi inclusi*.

The **cartella editoriale** is the standard page built on it: **30 lines of 60 battute**, which makes **1,800 battute**. Publishers, agencies, translators and editors all price and plan in cartelle.

| Unit | Is |
|---|---|
| 1 battuta | One character, spaces included |
| 1 cartella editoriale | 1,800 battute (30 × 60) |
| A novel of 250 cartelle | 450,000 battute |

> Other cartelle exist. Translation agencies and journalism sometimes use a cartella of 1,500 or of 2,000 battute. In book publishing, *cartella editoriale* means 1,800. When a contract or a call for submissions says *cartella*, check which one.

## Count in battute while you write

Click the count at the bottom of the page until it reads **characters with spaces**. Every count in the app then follows: the chapter, the book, the sidebar. Notes to yourself, scene titles and Markdown marks are not counted.

To know your cartelle at any moment, divide by 1,800.

## Make a manuscript in cartelle

1. In the book's settings, set the **Book language** to **Italiano**, and enter your **Contact (manuscript)**.
2. Press [[⌘E]] and choose **PDF**.
3. Choose the layout **Manuscript in cartelle (30 × 60)**.
4. Click **Export**.

Musset sets each page on the grid: 30 lines of 60 characters in Courier at 12 point, on A4, aligned on the left without hyphenation. Each page is filled to its thirty lines.

- The title page carries the title, your name, your contact details and the length: *circa 274 cartelle (493.000 battute)*.
- The header has your surname and the title, with the page number on the right.
- Chapters begin on a new page.

The figure on the title page is the number of battute divided by 1,800. The PDF itself has more pages than that, because real lines are rarely full: paragraphs end mid-line and dialogue is short.

## Italian typography, set for you

Musset applies Italian usage while you write: «caporali» with “virgolette alte” inside, the long dash for dialogue, the curly apostrophe. It also fixes two things typed on every Italian keyboard in a hurry: *E'* becomes *È*, and *po'* gets its proper apostrophe. See [automatic typography](/guide/smart-typography/).

## Common questions

**How many cartelle is a novel?** Italian houses often speak of novels from 150 to 400 cartelle. The publisher's or the prize's own rules are what count.

**How many words is a cartella?** Around 280 to 300 words of Italian prose.

**Spazi inclusi or esclusi?** Inclusi, unless stated otherwise.

**Must it be Courier?** A cartella needs a monospaced face so that every line holds 60 battute. Many publishers today accept any readable manuscript with the count of battute stated; when they ask for cartelle, this layout is what they mean.

**For a prize?** Fill in **Pseudonym (prizes)** in the export sheet for an anonymous manuscript, and rename the exported file, whose name contains the author's name.

Other countries have their own units: see [manuscript formats by country](/guide/manuscript-formats-by-country/).
