## What a milestone is

A milestone is the whole book as it stood on a day you chose. The [history](/guide/book-history/) already keeps every sitting; a milestone gives one of them a name you will recognise in a year: *First draft*, *Sent to the publisher*, *Before the rewrite*.

Set one:

- when a draft is done;
- before a change you are not sure about;
- the day you send the book to anyone, so you always know exactly what they read.

A milestone is also what your readers read. In Musset, a beta reader or an editor never reads your desk: they read a milestone you gave them. See [sharing a book with readers](/guide/share-with-beta-readers/).

## Set a milestone

1. Open the command bar ([[⌘K]]) and choose **Set a Milestone…**.
2. Give it a name. Musset suggests *First draft*, *Before the rewrite* and *Submitted*.
3. Add a note for later if it helps: who it went to, what you had just changed.
4. Click **Set the Milestone**.

The sheet shows the book's word count and how many words were written and cut since the last milestone, which is a fair measure of what a draft cost.

You can also set a milestone on a past moment: in the book's history, select a sitting and choose **Set a Milestone Here…**. It is how you name the draft you finished three weeks ago and forgot to mark.

> Musset sets a few milestones without being asked, before anything risky: *Before the corrections of* an editor when you bring in a Word file, *Before going back to* a date when you go back in the history, and *First reading* when you invite your first reader.

## See your milestones

Open the book's overview and choose the **Milestones** tab. Each milestone is a card with its date, its note, and who reads it. The top of the page shows what is on your desk today and which chapters you have touched since the latest milestone.

In the book's history ([[⌘K]], **The Book's History**), milestones are the flags above the timeline. Select one to see what has happened since: words written, words cut, chapters touched.

## Export the book as it was

A publisher asks for the version you sent in March. Select the milestone in the book's history and choose **Export the Book as It Was…**. The export sheet opens on the book as it stood that day, with every format available, and the milestone's name goes into the file name.

## Start again from a milestone

Choose **Start a Track from This Milestone…** to open a parallel manuscript that begins at the milestone, while your current text stays where it is. See [tracks](/guide/tracks/).

To take the manuscript itself back, use **Go Back to This Sitting…** in the [history](/guide/book-history/#go-back-to-a-past-sitting).

## Remove a milestone

On a milestone's card, open **…** and choose **Remove the Milestone…**. Only the flag goes: the text stays in the history. If readers were given that milestone, they have nothing to read until you give them another.
