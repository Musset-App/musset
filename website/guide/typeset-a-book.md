## A book, not a printout

A manuscript is a working document. A book is typeset: justified lines broken with care, hyphenation by the rules of the language, margins that allow for the binding, page numbers on the outside edge.

Musset does this itself. There is no template to choose and no settings panel. You get two editions.

| | PDF for readers | PDF for the printer |
|---|---|---|
| **For** | Reading on a screen or tablet; sending to friends | A printed book |
| **Page** | 120 × 188 mm | The trim size of your book's language |
| **Pages** | Single | Facing: left and right |
| **Cover** | Can be included | Never included; printers take the cover as a separate file |

Press [[⌘E]], choose **PDF**, and pick one.

## What the typesetting does

- **EB Garamond**, a revival of the sixteenth-century Garamond types, with its true italics, small capitals and old-style figures.
- **Paragraphs set as a whole.** Line breaks are chosen across the paragraph, not line by line, which avoids loose lines and rivers of white.
- **Hyphenation by language**: American and British English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, each by its own rules.
- **Chapter openings** on a new page, sunk from the top, with the title in small capitals.
- **Parts** on a page of their own, on a right-hand page.
- **Page numbers** at the foot, on the outer edge, in old-style figures; none on opening pages.
- **Scene breaks** as you chose them: a blank line, or an asterism (⁂).
- **Footnotes** at the foot of the page.

## The trim size of your market

Books are not the same size everywhere. The printer's edition uses a common trade format of your book's language:

| Book language | Trim size |
|---|---|
| English (US) | 5.5 × 8.5 in |
| English (UK) | 129 × 198 mm (B-format) |
| Français | 130 × 200 mm |
| Deutsch | 120 × 190 mm |
| Español, Português (Portugal) | 150 × 230 mm |
| Italiano, Português (Brasil) | 140 × 210 mm |

On facing pages the inside margin is wider than the outside one, to leave room for the binding.

The size follows the book's language, set in the book's settings. Use the **Single pages / Facing pages** switch in the export sheet to override the page arrangement for one export.

## Cover and special pages

Choose **File ▸ Cover & Special Pages** to write the pages around the text:

- a **cover**: typographic, with an image, or plain;
- the **title page**;
- a **dedication** and an **epigraph** with its source;
- the **contents**;
- **acknowledgements**;
- a **colophon** on the last page.

Tick the ones you want in the export sheet. The words Musset prints itself, such as *Contents* or *Acknowledgements*, are in the book's language.

## See it before you export

Press [[⇧⌘R]] for [Read mode](/guide/write-revise-read/): the typeset pages beside your text, for the chapter or the whole book, redrawn as you write. Double-click a place on the page to go to it in the text.

Reading your own book as a book changes what you see. Widows, a chapter that ends three lines into a page, a paragraph that runs two pages: you find them here, while they are still cheap to fix.

## Print on demand

The printer's edition is the **interior** file that print-on-demand services ask for: the pages at their final size, as a PDF with the fonts embedded.

Before you upload:

1. **Check the trim size.** Choose the same size on the service as your PDF's page. If the service does not offer your size, change the book's language variant to one whose size it does offer, or ask us about your case.
2. **Check the page count.** The service uses it to work out the width of the spine.
3. **Make the cover separately.** The printed cover is one wide file with the back, the spine and the front, made to the service's template. Musset does not make that file.
4. **Order a proof copy.** No screen shows what paper shows.

> The PDF is made at the trim size, without crop marks or bleed. That is what print-on-demand services expect for a text-only interior. An offset printer may ask for marks; tell them the file is at final size.

## Reading on a tablet

The reader's edition, 120 × 188 mm on single pages, suits a tablet and a computer screen. For an e-reader with a small screen, export an [EPUB](/guide/export-pdf-word-epub/#epub) instead: its text reflows to the device.

## What you cannot change, and why

There is no font menu and no margin slider. A typeset page is a set of decisions that depend on one another, and most adjustments by hand make it worse. Musset makes them once, properly, for each language.

If your book needs a design of its own, [export Word](/guide/export-pdf-word-epub/#word): its named styles flow into InDesign or a designer's template.
