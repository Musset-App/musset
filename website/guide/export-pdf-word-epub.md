## No Compile to learn

Getting a manuscript out of a writing app is often the hardest part of using it. Musset takes the opposite course: the layouts are made for you, correct for your book's language, and there is nothing to design.

Press [[⌘E]] (**Export…**). You choose three things: the format, what goes in, and the layout.

## The formats

| Format | Layouts | For |
|---|---|---|
| **PDF** | The submission manuscript of your country | Agents, publishers, prizes |
| | **PDF for readers** | Reading on a screen or a tablet, sending to friends |
| | **PDF for the printer** | A printed book, print on demand |
| **DOCX** | **Word (.docx)** | Editors and publishers who work in Word |
| **EPUB** | **EPUB** | E-readers |

The PDFs are typeset by Musset itself, with the same engine that draws [Read mode](/guide/write-revise-read/), so what you saw beside your text is what you get.

## What goes in

Under **Contents**, choose:

- **The whole book**: every chapter, in order;
- **Chapters by status**: for example only what is *Edited* or *Final*;
- **Chosen chapters**: tick them, for a sample, a reader, a submission of the first fifty pages.

Chapters you marked **Exclude from export** and chapters in Drafts are never included.

Under **Special pages**, tick the ones you want: **Cover**, **Title page**, **Dedication**, **Epigraph**, **Contents**, **Acknowledgements**. You write them once under **File ▸ Cover & Special Pages**.

**Between scenes** sets what the reader sees where a scene ends: nothing, a blank line, or an asterism (⁂). It is a setting of the book, used by every export.

The bottom of the sheet shows the count and, for a PDF, an estimate of the pages. Click **Export**; the file opens in the Finder.

## What is never exported

- Notes to yourself in the text (`<!-- … -->`) and margin notes.
- Scene titles: they are your working names.
- Corrections you have not decided. The export prints the text as it stands without them, so a half-reviewed chapter never goes out with struck words in it. See [corrections](/guide/track-changes/).

## Where the files go

Exports are saved in the book's folder, in `.musset/exports/`, and named so you can tell them apart months later: title, what was exported, author, layout, date and time.

```
THE_SALT_ROAD_EDITED_FINAL_CLAIRE_MOREL_STANDARD_MANUSCRIPT_2026-11-20_0912.pdf
```

Musset remembers your last choices for each book.

## Export a past draft

A publisher asks for exactly what you sent in March. Open the book's history, select the [milestone](/guide/milestones/), and choose **Export the Book as It Was…**. The same sheet opens on the book as it stood that day.

## Word

The Word file is laid out the way editors expect: A4, 2.5 cm margins, Times New Roman 12, line spacing 1.5, your name and the title in the header, page numbers at the foot.

It uses **named styles** throughout, for chapter titles, parts, scene breaks, block quotes, dedication and epigraph, with real footnotes. A publisher's template, or InDesign, takes it without cleaning.

**Track Changes is switched on** in the file. When the file comes back, [bring it in](/guide/word-track-changes/) and the editor's changes become corrections in your text.

## EPUB

The EPUB has one file per chapter, a table of contents, your dedication, epigraph and acknowledgements, and the book's language set for the e-reader's hyphenation and dictionary. Open it in Apple Books to check it, or send it to an e-reader.

For a store's own requirements, such as a cover image of a given size, check the file with the store's previewer before you upload it.

## Which one do I send?

| To | Send |
|---|---|
| A literary agent or a publisher | The [submission manuscript](/guide/manuscript-formats-by-country/) of their country, unless their guidelines ask for Word |
| A competition | The submission manuscript, with a pseudonym if the rules ask for anonymity |
| An editor you hired | Word, or an invitation to [read in Musset](/guide/share-with-beta-readers/) |
| Beta readers | An invitation to read in Musset, or the PDF for readers |
| A printer or print-on-demand service | [The PDF for the printer](/guide/typeset-a-book/) |
| Your own e-reader | EPUB |
