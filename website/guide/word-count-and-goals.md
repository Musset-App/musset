## The count at the bottom of the page

The pill at the bottom right of the page shows the length of the open chapter. Rest the pointer on it for the book's total and what you wrote today.

**Click the count to change its unit**, for the whole app:

- **words**, the measure of English-language publishing;
- **characters**, without spaces;
- **characters with spaces**, the measure of French, German, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese publishers.

Musset counts the text of the book and nothing else: scene markers, notes to yourself and Markdown marks are left out. Chapters you excluded from export are not in the book's total.

When the time comes to tell a publisher how long the book is, each country counts its own way: words rounded to the hundred, *feuillets*, *Normseiten*, *cartelle*. Musset does those sums in the export. See [manuscript formats by country](/guide/manuscript-formats-by-country/).

## What you wrote today

*Today* is the net number of words added to this book since you first opened it today. Cutting a paragraph lowers it, which is honest; it never goes below zero.

## Set a goal for the book

1. Choose **Book Settings…** ([[⌥⌘,]]).
2. Under **General**, enter a **Word goal**: 80,000 for a first novel, for example.

The book's overview and the Stats panel then show where you stand: the percentage reached and the words left.

## The Stats panel

Press [[⌘4]] (**View ▸ Stats**). Nothing here comes from an AI; it is all counted on your Mac.

| Figure | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **words** | The chapter's length |
| **reading time** | At 230 words a minute |
| **in the book** | The whole book |
| **today** | Net words today |
| **words per sentence** | The chapter's average |
| **longest sentence** | In words |
| **dialogue** | The share of the chapter spoken aloud |
| **paragraphs** · **scenes** | How the chapter is cut |

Below is the book, chapter by chapter: one bar per chapter, as tall as the chapter is long, in the colour of its status. A chapter twice the height of its neighbours is worth a look, and so is a run of very short ones.

## How long should a book be?

Publishers think in ranges, not rules, and the ranges differ by genre. As rough guides for English-language adult fiction: a novel usually runs 70,000 to 100,000 words, a novella 20,000 to 40,000, and fantasy and historical novels run longer. A first novel far outside its genre's range is harder to place, in either direction.

The useful number is the one in the submission guidelines of the publisher or agent you are writing to.
