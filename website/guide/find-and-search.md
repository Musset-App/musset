## Find in the chapter

Press [[⌘F]]. A bar opens across the top of the page; type at least two letters. If you had selected a word, it is already there.

- Every match lights up in yellow, and the bar counts them: *3 / 12*.
- [[↩]] or [[⌘G]] goes to the next match, [[⇧↩]] or [[⇧⌘G]] to the previous one. The search wraps around the chapter.
- [[Esc]] closes the bar and returns you to the text.

Searches ignore capitals and accents: *eleonore* finds *Éléonore*. That is what you want in a manuscript, where the question is usually "where did I mention her?".

## Replace

Press [[⌥⌘F]], or click **Replace** in the find bar.

- **Replace** changes the current match and moves to the next.
- **Replace All** changes every match in the chapter, as one step of undo.

Replace works on the open chapter. To change a character's name through the whole book, use [Rename](/guide/rename-a-character/) instead: it shows you every line before it changes, and covers notes and sheets too.

## Search the whole book

Press [[⇧⌘F]] (**Search the Book…**). The sidebar becomes a search panel.

- Results are grouped by chapter, in the order of the book, with the match in its sentence.
- Click a result to open the chapter with the match selected. [[↩]] opens the first one.
- Tick **All Books** to search every book in your library. Useful when you know you wrote that image somewhere.

You can also start from the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/): press [[⌘K]], type the words, and choose the last row, *Search in the text*.

## Search what you deleted

The search above looks at the book as it is today. To find a sentence you cut last month, use the search field of the History panel ([[⌘7]]): it reads **Search, even what no longer exists**. See [the book's history](/guide/book-history/#find-a-sentence-that-no-longer-exists).

## Go to a place by name

To jump to a chapter or a titled scene, press [[⌘K]] and type part of its name. It is faster than scrolling the sidebar, and it reaches your other books too.
