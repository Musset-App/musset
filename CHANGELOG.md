# Changelog

What changed in each release of Musset, newest first. Musset updates itself; every version is also at [musset.app/download](https://musset.app/download) and on the [releases page](https://github.com/Musset-App/musset/releases).

## 0.7.2 — 2026-11-28

- With OpenRouter or another server that needs a key, a missing key is now said plainly ("No key for OpenRouter on this Mac"), and Settings › AI › Providers shows "no key" instead of "connected". Nothing is sent without one.
- Errors from your own server no longer tell you to sign in to Claude or Codex.
- Fixed: the critic table sent Codex's model setting to your own server instead of the model chosen for the AI reader.

## 0.7.1 — 2026-11-28

- Send Feedback, at the foot of the sidebar: an idea, a problem or a question goes straight to the people who make Musset, not to a public page. Nothing from your books is sent.
- In the chat, each answer now says which model gave it, and the menu under the message box picks the model for the next one, so you can compare two models on the same passage.
- Fixed: with your own model, the chat could answer with the server's default model instead of the one you chose, and notes from its answers were signed by Claude.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-27

- Your own model: OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio or any compatible server, beside Claude and ChatGPT. Add them in Settings › AI › Providers.
- Each task now has its own provider and model: Claude for the conversation, a model on OpenRouter for the margin notes, Ollama for proofreading.
- With Ollama or LM Studio, the model runs on your Mac and the book never leaves it.
- A model browser lists every model a server offers, with its price and how much it reads at once.

## 0.6.1 — 2026-11-24

- Bullet and numbered lists are on in every new book, whatever its form. In a book you already have, switch them on in the book's settings, under Structure, In the text.
- Where lists are on, a hyphen line that reads as a line of dialogue still becomes a dialogue dash; a list item stays an item.
- Changing a book's form keeps the list settings you chose.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-11-24

- A new first page: type your book's title, press Return, and write. Genre, language and where the book is kept are one click away, and the folder you choose is shown.
- An example book to look around in: The Time Machine as a book in progress, with chapters at several stages, character and place sheets, margin notes and milestones. In English, French, German, Italian and Spanish.
- Scrivener import reads real projects: paragraphs stay paragraphs, footnotes, comments and annotations are kept, parts and Scrivener 2 projects are understood.
- Bold text is bold in Write mode, and ***bold italic*** works there and in the PDF.
- Choosing Essay starts a book with bullet lists, numbered lists and sub-headings on. In any book, they are in the book's settings, under Structure, In the text.
- The list of genres is in your language.
- The disk image opens on a window that shows the way to Applications.

## 0.5.1 — 2026-11-19

- Share a part of a book: when you invite a reader, give them the whole book, only the chapters at the statuses you choose, or chapters you pick one by one. Change it later from the Readers page. On iPad, Musset Reader's text is larger.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-19

- Milestones, a page of the book: the manuscript on your desk, which only you see, then each milestone with who reads it.
- Readers read a milestone you chose, never the text as you write it. Give each reader a milestone, and a newer one when you are ready.
- Readers, a page of the book: who reads it, invitations still out, your circle one click away, the reviews to read.
- Inviting someone is one sheet, from the Share button in the title bar or from the command bar.
- The book’s settings are a page of its home, beside Readers.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-16

- Scene cards: a chapter's scenes side by side, each with its whole text; move, split, merge and rename them.
- The book at a glance: every chapter with its opening and its close, by part and by chapter; counts in words or characters.
- Book structure: forms, level names and numbering, strands; lists and sub-headings.
- Boards: maps with search, satellite and street view; arrows you can select and style; groups; rename in place.
- Fixes: Undo and Redo in the editor, a crash on Undo, the version chip on every chapter.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-14

- Panes and tabs: write a chapter beside your notes, your Bible or a cork board. Several chapters open at once, and windows of their own.
- A live Bible: the cast tracks itself as you write, sheets keep what the book says, a character is renamed everywhere in one step, and each place has its map.
- Cork boards with pens, shapes, arrows and cards.
- One Overview for the summary, the synopsis and the grid. The book's history and the AI reader each have one place.
- Typing stays fast in long chapters, and books open sooner.
- A sentence you delete goes to the Cuts. Undo stays with its tab.

## 0.2.1 — 2026-11-12

- Fixes Musset quitting on its own when a sync's connection died on a slow network.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-11

- Musset Reader for iPhone and iPad, and your circle: editors and beta readers annotate, notes become conversations.
- Reading mode sets the book as printed, with everyone's marks.
- Return, Return, Return: a paragraph, a scene, a chapter.
- French manuscripts in Garamond on A4.
- Updates arrive on their own from now on.
