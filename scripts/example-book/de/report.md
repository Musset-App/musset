# Die Zeitmaschine (Greve, J. C. C. Bruns, Minden 1904): how the text was made

Source: the scan of the 1904 edition at the Universitäts- und Landesbibliothek Münster (urn:nbn:de:hbz:6:1-123919,
Public Domain Mark 1.0). The text in `Musset/Resources/Example/example-time-machine-de.md` is what that scan prints:
30,867 words, sixteen chapters and an Epilog, 307 paragraphs, one footnote.

## Method
- Four readings of every page by the Mac's own text recognition (different settings and model revisions), aligned
  word by word: 36,107 positions, 779 disagreements.
- A modern ebook of the same translation was aligned as a comparison aid only. No word of the text comes from it;
  where it differed from the scan, the page image decided and the 1904 form was kept.
- About 600 printed lines were read on the page image. A proofreading read of the whole text found no wrong word.

## Where an error could have survived
- The scan carries pencil notes on about 60 pages and much underlining; underlines read as dashes, commas and full
  stops. 24 such marks were removed on the image. A stray comma under an underline could remain.
- 79 commas present in the scan and absent from the comparison text were accepted without looking at the image.
- All four readings come from one engine and share its errors (ß read as l, guillemets lost).

## Kept as printed
Spelling, with its inconsistencies (Hülfe, Thames, Entwickelung, Billett, Fascination, ss for ß in Genuss, müsste,
dreissig); the quotation marks »…« and ‚…‘; the year 802,701 always in words. Spaced-out type is given as *…*.

## Misprints corrected
Seven, listed in `printfixes.txt` (printed form, corrected form, page).

## Uncertain after looking at the image
- p. 65/66 of the PDF: no mark after « Not » in « Die Kraft ist das Ergebnis der Not die Sicherheit … »; left as printed.
- p. 70: the second comma in « der letzte, große Frieden, begann » may be pencil; kept.
- p. 16: « begannen » printed without umlaut; kept.
- p. 77: no quotation marks seen at « Das Schlimmste angenommen? sagte ich. »

## The 1951 reprint was revised
The comparison text differs from the 1904 printing in about 600 places: some 65 changed words, 110 words added,
dropped or moved (whole sentences are missing from it), 190 small spelling and grammar changes, 170 of punctuation.
The 1904 text bundled here is therefore not the one in circulation as an ebook.
